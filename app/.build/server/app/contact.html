Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about camping with us

What should I bring for camping?

We provide fire rings, picnic tables, and restroom facilities. You'll need to bring your tent, sleeping gear, food, and personal items. Check our packing checklist on our website!

Are pets allowed?

Yes! Well-behaved pets are welcome. They must be kept on leash and owners are responsible for cleanup. Pet fee is $10 per night.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Cancellations made 48 hours before arrival receive full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to one night's fee.

Is there cell phone service?

Cell service is limited in some areas of the campground. We have WiFi available at the main lodge for emergencies and check-in.
